Terms of Engagement

These terms govern the engagement between the Client and the Agency for social media services. By proceeding, both parties agree to the following.

01 Client Responsibilities

  • Provide brand information, logos, guidelines, and product details before commencement.
  • Grant account access and approve content within agreed timelines.
  • Notify the Agency immediately of any requirement changes.
  • Ensure all provided information is accurate and complete.

02 Payment Terms

  • 50% advance required before work begins; balance before final delivery or per milestone.
  • Late payments may attract additional charges; work may be paused until cleared.

03 Pricing

  • Quoted prices exclude additional expenses unless stated otherwise.
  • Services requested post-confirmation billed separately.

04 Revisions

  • Up to 2 revisions included per deliverable.
  • Additional revisions and major post-approval changes billed separately or treated as a new project.

05 Project Timeline

  • Timelines commence only after advance payment and receipt of all required assets.
  • Client delays will extend the delivery schedule accordingly.

06 Content Approval

  • All content must be approved by the Client before publishing.
  • The Agency is not responsible for errors or omissions once approval is given.

07 Social Media Performance

  • The Agency does not guarantee followers, engagement, reach, sales, or virality.
  • Performance depends on platform algorithms and audience behaviour.

08 Intellectual Property

  • Content remains the Agency's property until full payment is received.
  • Ownership transfers to the Client after full payment, unless otherwise agreed in writing.

09 Portfolio Rights

The Agency may showcase completed work in its portfolio, website, or social media. Clients may request confidentiality in writing to opt out.

10 Third-Party Services

  • Paid ads, influencer fees, printing, music licences, stock assets, models, makeup artists, and venue rentals are excluded unless specified in the agreement.
  • Props required for the shoot are not included.
  • Travelling costs are not included in the deal. Travelling costs for shoots will need to be provided/borne separately by the Client.

11 Cancellation Policy

  • Advance payment is non-refundable.
  • If cancelled after work begins, payment for completed work is due.
  • The Agency may terminate due to non-payment, misconduct, or repeated delays.

12 Refund Policy

No refunds after work has commenced unless agreed by the Agency in writing.

13 Confidentiality

Both parties agree to keep all confidential information private throughout and after the engagement.

14 Account Access

  • Client must provide correct login credentials before work begins.
  • The Agency is not responsible for delays caused by missing or incorrect access.

15 Communication

  • Email, WhatsApp, phone calls, and signed documents are accepted channels.
  • Approvals through these channels are considered valid and binding.

16 Force Majeure

The Agency is not liable for delays caused by natural disasters, government restrictions, internet or power failures, pandemics, strikes, or events beyond reasonable control.

17 Limitation of Liability

The Agency is not liable for business losses, account bans, hacking not caused by Agency negligence, algorithm changes, or platform failures.
