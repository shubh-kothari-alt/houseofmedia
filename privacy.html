Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how HOMe — The House Of Media collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you visit our website or engage with our services.

01 Information We Collect

We only collect the information necessary to provide our services and improve your experience. This includes:

  • Information you provide directly: When you contact us via WhatsApp, email, or direct message, we collect your name, contact details, and any project-specific information you share.
  • Automatically collected data: While we currently prioritize a minimalist visual portfolio without aggressive tracking, basic technical data (like browser type, device information, and standard server logs) may be collected automatically when you visit our site to ensure proper functionality and security.

02 How We Use Information

We use the information we collect for the following operational and business purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries, schedule consultations, and discuss potential collaborations.
  • To provide, operate, and maintain the services you request from us.
  • To send administrative information, updates regarding projects, and invoices.
  • To improve the design, security, and functionality of our digital presence.

03 Sharing & Disclosure

We respect your privacy and do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We only share information under the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We may share data with trusted third-party vendors (such as hosting platforms or invoicing software) who assist us in operating our agency, subject to strict confidentiality obligations.
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required to do so by law, or in response to valid requests by public authorities.
  • Business Transfers: In the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, client information may be transferred as a business asset.

04 Data Security

We implement reasonable and standard security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

05 Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have specific rights regarding your personal data. Generally, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of any inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal and operational retention requirements.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details provided below.

06 Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external platforms, such as Instagram or WhatsApp. We are not responsible for the privacy practices, tracking methods, or content of these third-party websites. We encourage you to read the privacy policies of any site you visit upon leaving ours.

07 Policy Updates

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices, services, or legal requirements. When we make updates, we will amend the date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically to stay informed about how we are protecting your information.
